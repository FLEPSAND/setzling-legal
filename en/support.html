Frequently asked questions

The pause doesn’t appear.

In Setzling, check under Settings that Screen Time is allowed and the pause is switched on, and on the plant’s page that apps are selected. Screen Time must be turned on under iOS Settings → Screen Time.

No notification arrives after “Open briefly”.

Allow notifications for Setzling under iOS Settings → Notifications → Setzling. Without notifications you can also open Setzling yourself; the pause then starts there.

Why don’t I see app names in Setzling?

Apple only passes the chosen apps to Setzling in encrypted form. This protects your privacy. You see the names in Apple’s app picker.

How do I get more plants?

With Setzling Pro, tap “New plant” in your garden, choose a group like social media or games, a plant and the matching apps. Each plant then grows with the time spent in its own apps.

How do I cancel Setzling Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Setzling Pro. Or in Setzling under Settings → “Manage subscription”.

I bought Pro but it isn’t active.

Tap “Restore” on the Pro page. Setzling then asks Apple for your purchases.

How do I remove the pause completely?

Switch off the pause in Setzling or delete the app. All pauses disappear with the app.
