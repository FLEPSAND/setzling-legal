Setzling

Less phone, more life.

Setzling puts a short pause in front of the apps that eat most of your time. Before TikTok, Instagram or a game opens, your plant asks you: do you really want to?

Every “No thanks” makes your plant grow, from seedling to blooming bush. And if you do want to open the app, you take a short breath and then have 15 minutes.

How it works

  • You choose the apps you want to open less often.
  • When you open one of them, the pause comes first.
  • Your plant grows every day, the most on good days. Watering once a day gives a little boost. It can never die.
  • Reminders in the morning and evening help you stay on track.

Setzling Pro

The basics are free. With Pro a whole garden grows: every group of apps gets its own plant, whether sunflower, cactus or little apple tree. On top of that you get schedules, an adjustable pause, a daily limit for unlocks and the weekly review.

Your data

Setzling has no account and no server. Everything stays on your iPhone. More in the privacy policy.
