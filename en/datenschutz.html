2. At a glance

Setzling works without an account. I don’t run a server for the app and receive no data from you. Everything you enter in Setzling stays on your iPhone. There is no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no profiling.

3. Data on your device

Stored locally in the app’s protected storage:

  • your answers from the onboarding (screen time estimate, goals) and the names of your plants
  • settings such as pause length, schedules and reminder times
  • the history of your plants: growth, watering, “No thanks”, unlocks and minutes of use per day
  • whether Pro applies and until when

Part of this is kept in an app group so that Setzling’s extensions (pause screen and background monitoring) can access it. This storage, too, is only on your device. If you delete the app, all data is deleted with it.

Legal basis: processing takes place exclusively on your device; nothing is transmitted to me.

4. Screen Time (Family Controls)

For the pause, Setzling uses Apple’s Screen Time interfaces (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity). Setzling receives the apps you choose only as encrypted identifiers, not as names. Apple only tells the app whether a time threshold has been reached (for example 30 minutes in the apps of one plant), not what you do there. This data does not leave your device.

You grant this permission yourself and can withdraw it at any time under iOS Settings → Screen Time.

5. Notifications

Setzling schedules reminders and notes on your device. There are no push servers and no data is sent. You can turn notifications off in the app and in iOS Settings.

6. Purchases

The basic features are free. “Setzling Pro” can be bought as a subscription or a one-time purchase. Payment is handled exclusively by Apple via your Apple Account. I receive no payment data, no address and no name. The app only asks Apple whether a valid purchase exists and stores the result with an expiry date on the device. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. This website

These pages are provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address. No cookies are set and no analytics services are used. Legal basis: legitimate interest in an accessible website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8. Contact by e-mail

If you write to me, I use your address and message only to reply and delete them when they are no longer needed.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). As I receive no data from the app, I generally hold no data about you. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Baden-Württemberg (Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz Baden-Württemberg).

10. Children

Setzling is not specifically aimed at children under 13 and does not collect data about them.
